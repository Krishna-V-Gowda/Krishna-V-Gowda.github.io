Proposition

A browser-local analytical workspace for profiling, visual diagnostics, transformations, feature selection, and PCA—and the real product subject corrected through TrustBench.

What is technically difficult

The product has to remain usable while exposing assumptions that analytical interfaces usually hide: duplicate headers, missing-token semantics, skew estimator choice, association definitions, leakage screening, and preprocessing order.

Evidence

Product surfaceLocal-first CSV workflow with diagnostics, transformations, selection, PCA, and exports.
Corrected semanticsDuplicate-header preservation, adjusted Fisher–Pearson skewness, identifier protection, order-invariant Cramér’s V, target-aware association, exact proxy leakage, all-predictor PCA.
Regression proof7 exact-source Node regressions.
Independent evidenceTrustBench post-repair result: 15 passes, one limitation, zero failures.

Where the claim stops

Exploratory analytical support. Health/readiness scores are transparent heuristics; association is not causation or model validity.

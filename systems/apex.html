The hard part

The product has to remain usable while exposing assumptions that analytical interfaces usually hide: duplicate headers, missing-token semantics, skew estimator choice, association definitions, leakage screening, and preprocessing order.

The evidence

Product surface
Local-first CSV workflow with diagnostics, transformations, selection, PCA, and exports.
Corrected semantics
Duplicate-header preservation, adjusted Fisher–Pearson skewness, identifier protection, order-invariant Cramér’s V, target-aware association, exact proxy leakage, all-predictor PCA.
Regression proof
7 exact-source Node regressions.
Independent evidence
TrustBench post-repair result: 15 passes, one limitation, zero failures.

Where the claim stops

Exploratory analytical support. Health/readiness scores are transparent heuristics; association is not causation or model validity.

Proposition

A walk-forward laboratory for long-only allocation under covariance error, regime changes, tail risk, turnover, and transaction costs.

What is technically difficult

Portfolio rules are easy to make attractive in-sample. RiskLab forces strict trailing windows, weight drift, costs, optimizer accounting, scenario controls, and paired moving-block-bootstrap comparisons before interpreting a result.

Evidence

Research surfaceSix allocation rules and four deterministic scenarios.
Controlled data1,800 × 8 heavy-tailed synthetic return panel across four regimes.
Uncertainty500 paired circular moving-block-bootstrap resamples per nonbaseline comparison.
Integrity29 tests and zero retained optimizer failures; adverse base path retained.

Where the claim stops

Synthetic research evidence only. No historical alpha, live trading, investment suitability, or recommendation.

Dayflow

Every workday, intelligently aligned.

An explainable workforce system that joins attendance, leave, workforce and payroll records, so HR can see what a decision does to the team before making it.

Inspect source

Engineering, the next two weeks. Tap a day to request leave.

The hard part

Most HR systems record a decision after it happens. Dayflow’s question comes first: what happens if we approve this? Its decision engine reads the team, every approved and pending absence that overlaps the requested dates, and recalculates coverage from stored records. It returns current and projected coverage, the change, a risk call and its reasons in plain sentences. It never approves anything on its own; HR decides, and the decision is kept in an audit trail.

The evidence

Decision rules
High risk below 60% projected coverage or with three or more people away; moderate below 80% or when another pending request overlaps; otherwise low. The panel above runs these exact rules.
Explainable signals
Attendance flags name the rule that fired: a missing checkout, a shift under four hours, a shift over ten. No lateness score is invented without a configured work-time baseline.
Boundaries
JWT authentication and server-side roles: an employee asking for the HR-only staff list gets HTTP 403.
Integrity
Automated backend tests, including payroll that refuses deductions above pay and rounds net pay to the paisa.

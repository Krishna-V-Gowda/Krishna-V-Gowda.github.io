Proposition

A verification-first settlement close in which AI ranks damaged evidence while deterministic financial controls and explicit human authority retain the right to close.

What is technically difficult

The hard problem is not fuzzy matching. It is preserving financial truth while allowing a model to help with ambiguity. Proofloom separates candidate ranking, policy authority, human review, and audit into inspectable layers.

Evidence

Hero batch185 synthetic records across payments, refunds, settlements, bank lines, and webhook deliveries.
Failure proofA 137-paise composition mismatch blocks the close before candidate ownership.
Held-out artifact0.9738 PR-AUC; 99.18% pair precision; 76.79% hybrid straight-through; merchant-group-held-out synthetic split.
Behavior suite20 tests in the integrated public release, including money, audit, outage, idempotency, API, policy, and adapter controls.

Where the claim stops

Synthetic records and generated labels only. No live merchant validation, production ROI, real-money execution, or regulatory certification.

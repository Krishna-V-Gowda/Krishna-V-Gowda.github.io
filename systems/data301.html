The hard part

Educational software can become decorative. DATA301 connects visual interaction to inspectable TypeScript algorithms and uses exact examples plus metamorphic properties to protect the underlying mathematics.

The evidence

Interactive surface
12 implemented laboratories across regression, classification, clustering, PCA, optimization, trees, SVM, curves, and ensembles.
Source protection
60 source tests, including 7 metamorphic properties checked over seeded random datasets.
Release gate
Source-integrity verification, 60 tests, typecheck, production build, public/private scan, and dependency audit.
Boundary design
Sanitized public edition with explicit authorization and private-storage architecture.

Where the claim stops

Educational two-dimensional algorithms and synthetic examples; no production cohort, concurrency, or model-serving claim.
